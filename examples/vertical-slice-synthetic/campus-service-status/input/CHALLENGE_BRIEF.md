# Synthetic challenge brief: Campus Service Access Challenge

> **SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — fictional event brief. Do not cite as a real competition.**

A fictional university innovation office invites teams to propose a lightweight digital prototype that helps students plan visits to campus administrative service counters.

## Fictional challenge constraints

- Prototype should be demonstrable in a short presentation.
- Teams should explain the user problem and show a simple workflow.
- A low-cost, software-only prototype is acceptable.
- The concept must not expose student personal records.
- No real integration, API access, budget, judging weights, registration date, eligibility rule, or IP term is supplied in this test brief.

## Fictional prompt

Explore ways to reduce uncertainty for students who need to visit a campus service counter. Do not assume that long queues, wasted trips, or demand for an app have been established by real research.
