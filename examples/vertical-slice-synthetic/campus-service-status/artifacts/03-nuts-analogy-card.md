# NUTS Exploration — Analogy Decision

> **SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — illustrative reasoning exercise, not a researched novelty claim.**

## Target function

Help a person decide whether to approach a service point now or wait, using a trustworthy indication of the current operating state.

## Cross-domain candidate

A public status/arrival display (for example, a transit information board) suggests the general mechanism of making operational state visible before a person reaches a point of service.

## Abstract principle

Provide a timely, understandable status signal at the point where a user makes a timing or routing decision.

## Transfer limitations

- A campus counter may not have reliable live data.
- Staff update burden may outweigh the benefit.
- A stale status can mislead users and damage trust.
- The pattern is not novel by itself; similar status displays are common in many domains.
- No specific transit system was researched for this synthetic fixture; no factual claim about performance is made.

## NUTS decision

**RETAIN ONLY AS A DESIGN HYPOTHESIS — NOT AS PROOF OF NOVELTY.** The transferable principle is useful enough to test, but there is no basis to claim originality or superiority. Prior-art and source research would be required for a real project.
