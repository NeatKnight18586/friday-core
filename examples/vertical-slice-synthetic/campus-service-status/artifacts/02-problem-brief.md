# Problem Brief — Campus Service Status

> **SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — no real student or staff research has been conducted.**

## 1. Target users

Hypothesized primary users: students who visit administrative service counters. Potential operational stakeholders: counter staff or service administrators.

## 2. Job / desired progress

When planning a service visit, a student may want to know whether the service is currently available and whether visiting now is worthwhile.

## 3. Current behavior and alternatives

**Unknown.** The fixture provides no observation of actual behavior and no inventory of existing campus communication channels.

## 4. Problem statement

A testable problem hypothesis is that some students may lack timely, reliable information about counter availability before making a visit. The importance, frequency, and current alternatives are not established.

## 5. Evidence

| Finding | Evidence type | Record | Confidence | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The fictional challenge asks for service-visit planning help | Synthetic source fact | CLM-0001 / SRC-0001 | High within fixture | Not a real event |
| Students experience consequential uncertainty | Inference / unverified | CLM-0002 | Low | No interviews or observations |
| A status display would change behavior | Prediction / unverified | CLM-0003 | Low | No prototype or usage test |

## 6. Opportunity

Investigate whether clear, current service-status information would meaningfully improve visit planning for a defined user group.

## 7. Research still needed

- Ask about recent, specific service visits rather than hypothetical app interest.
- Include relevant counter staff to test update workload and operational fit.
- Map existing announcements, queue tools, and service processes.
- Record consent and avoid collecting identifiers or sensitive case details.

## 8. Disposition

**PROVISIONAL OPPORTUNITY — VALIDATE.** Desk/synthetic evidence is not user validation.
