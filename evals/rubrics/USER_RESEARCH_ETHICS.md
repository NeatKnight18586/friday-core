# User Research Ethics and Participant Care Rubric

Use when Peanuts proposes interviews, observation, usability tests, surveys, recordings, or field pilots involving people. Apply local law, institutional rules, and domain-specific safeguards; this rubric is not an ethics-board approval.

| Dimension | 1 — Poor | 3 — Adequate | 5 — Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose and necessity | Collects data without a clear question | Purpose exists but data needs are broad | Research question and minimum necessary data are explicit |
| Recruitment and inclusion | Convenience sample misrepresented | Recruitment limitations disclosed | Participant criteria, inclusion/exclusion, accessibility and sampling limitations are clear |
| Consent and autonomy | No meaningful consent or withdrawal path | Basic information and agreement | Voluntary informed consent, purpose, recording, use, withdrawal and contact route addressed |
| Privacy and retention | Identifiable data collected/shared casually | Some safeguards | Data minimization, access, storage, retention/deletion and tool-sharing restrictions addressed |
| Vulnerability and oversight | Ignores minors or power imbalance | Recognizes obvious concerns | Escalates to appropriate institutional/qualified oversight and avoids proceeding prematurely |
| Analysis and reporting | Invents or overgeneralizes findings | Limitations noted | Quotes/observations, coding, negative cases, sample and inference limits are traceable |

Critical failure: fabricated consent/approval, unauthorized identifiable-data transfer, coercive participation, or research involving vulnerable participants without required safeguards.
