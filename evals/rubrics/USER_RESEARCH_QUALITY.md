# User Research Quality Rubric

| Dimension | 1 — deficient | 3 — adequate/provisional | 5 — strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research question | Leading or solution-confirming | Clear but broad | Neutral, decision-linked, answerable |
| Participant fit | Convenience only, no rationale | Relevant group and basic criteria | Selection tied to experience, segments, inclusion, and bias are explicit |
| Method fit | Method cannot answer question | Plausible exploratory method | Method, sample and inference limits fit the question |
| Ethics and consent | Consent/data safeguards absent | Consent and data plan noted | Informed consent, accessibility, minimization, access, retention, withdrawal and escalation handled |
| Session quality | Leading or hypothetical intent only | Some past-behavior prompts | Neutral, concrete, context-rich prompts with evidence/artifact opportunities |
| Synthesis integrity | Quotes/findings invented or overgeneralized | Themes and caveats present | Observation/statement/interpretation separated; contrary cases and segments preserved |

A high score does not replace institutional, legal, safeguarding or ethics review where required.
