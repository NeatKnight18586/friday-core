# PRD and Requirements Quality Rubric

Use this rubric to review a product definition and its requirement records. It is a structured judgement aid, not a claim of certification.

| Dimension | Strong evidence | Weak / failing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Problem definition | Specific context, affected users, current behavior, consequences, and evidence limits. | Solution-first language, generic pain points, or invented user needs. |
| Outcomes | Measurable user/product outcomes, baseline or explicit baseline gap, timeframe and guardrails. | Feature counts or vanity metrics presented as value. |
| Scope | Small coherent release slice, explicit non-goals, exclusions and deferred scope. | Kitchen-sink MVP and unprioritized feature lists. |
| Requirement quality | Atomic, necessary, unambiguous, feasible, traceable, uniquely identified and owned. | Vague, duplicate, solution-prescriptive or orphan requirements. |
| Evidence maturity | Origin and evidence status are explicit; assumptions and constraints remain visible. | Desk research/personas presented as direct validation; unsupported certainty. |
| Compliance | Applicable mandatory rules are current, authoritative and non-waivable without qualified disposition. | Unverified legal claims, policy assumptions or schedule-driven waiver. |
| Acceptance criteria | Observable conditions and explicit pass thresholds; criteria cover meaningful edge cases. | Subjective adjectives or requirements with no verification route. |
| Change control | Dependencies, conflicts, supersession, version and owner decisions are recorded. | Silent scope drift or overwritten decisions. |

A PRD is not approved merely because the document is complete. Owner approval is separate from model review. Any unresolved critical risk, mandatory compliance failure, unsupported mandatory requirement, or missing acceptance coverage blocks approval.
