# Event Intelligence Quality Rubric

| Dimension | 1 — deficient | 3 — adequate/provisional | 5 — strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Edition/source accuracy | Old or third-party information treated as current | Edition checked for most facts | Current official edition and key sections directly inspected |
| Eligibility and deadlines | Material rules omitted or timezone invented | Major constraints recorded with some gaps | Eligibility, team limits and dated deadlines/timezones trace to official sources or explicit unknowns |
| Deliverables and judging | Requirements guessed or weights invented | Main deliverables/criteria found | Exact official requirements and published weights faithfully captured; absent weights clearly stated |
| Technology/IP/AI rules | Restrictions ignored | Major rules captured | Technology, data, IP, licensing, attribution and AI-use requirements checked |
| Conflict handling | Conflict silently resolved | Conflict noted | Material conflict is preserved, risk explained, and organizer question prepared |
| Decision readiness | Unknown treated as permission | Unknowns listed | Compliance gates and product-fit overlay are separate; exit criteria are explicit |

A critical eligibility or IP ambiguity is a gate and must not be hidden in an average score.
