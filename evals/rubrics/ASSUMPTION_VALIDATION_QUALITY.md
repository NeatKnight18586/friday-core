# Assumption Validation Quality Rubric (B7)

| Dimension | 1 — Poor | 3 — Adequate | 5 — Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assumption priority | Tests easy/low-value beliefs while decisive assumptions remain hidden | Some consequence/uncertainty reasoning | Prioritizes by consequence if wrong, uncertainty, and value of information |
| Falsifiability | Hypothesis is vague or cannot be disproved | Observable but threshold is weak | Hypothesis, success/failure signals and decision threshold are predeclared |
| Method fit | Method cannot answer the question | Method is plausible with limitations | Method directly addresses the assumption with proportionate scope |
| Ethics and data controls | Consent/privacy/ethics ignored | Basic controls recorded | Consent, minimization, de-identification, local review and retention are appropriate |
| Evidence traceability | No verifiable result or provenance | Some result evidence | Completed outcome links to evidence, limitations and protocol deviations |
| Interpretation | Planned test treated as result or overgeneralized | Result mostly cautious | Result compared with threshold; uncertainty and generalization limits explicit |

A completed test is not automatically a validated assumption. Distinguish sample findings, technical demonstration, expert opinion, desk research and real-world behavior.
