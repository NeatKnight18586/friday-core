# Evidence and Method Fidelity Rubric

| Dimension | 1 — Poor | 3 — Adequate | 5 — Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source fit | Uses weak/inappropriate sources | Sources are broadly relevant | Primary/authoritative source chosen where appropriate |
| Recency/context | Ignores version/date/context | Notes some limitations | Verifies edition, recency and applicability |
| Claim provenance | Unsupported claims or mixed evidence types | Major claims mostly traced | Fact, user report, observation, inference and prediction clearly separated |
| Method prerequisites | Runs method without prerequisites | Notes some missing inputs | Correctly applies, adapts, defers or rejects method based on prerequisites |
| Limitations | Omits limitations | Generic caveats | Specific sampling, source, method and transfer limitations are clear |
| Actionability | Recommendations do not follow evidence | Some useful actions | Clear next tests/decisions follow from evidence |

A high score requires evidence in the output, not merely correct terminology.
