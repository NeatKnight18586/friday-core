# Research Plan Quality Rubric

Score each applicable dimension from 1–5; use `UNKNOWN / NOT ASSESSED` when evidence is insufficient. Score is not confidence.

| Dimension | 1 — deficient | 3 — adequate/provisional | 5 — strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision focus | No decision or unfocused browsing | Decision and main questions stated | Questions are prioritized by decision impact and uncertainty |
| Source strategy | No source hierarchy | Relevant source classes identified | Primary sources, discovery indexes, counterevidence and version checks are planned |
| Coverage | Important gaps hidden | Coverage and unknowns recorded | Critical questions have evidence or explicit blocker/test; scope is reproducible |
| Evidence integrity | Snippets/AI output treated as proof | Sources and claim status linked | Claim-level traceability, source limitations, conflicts and source dependence recorded |
| Method fit | Method unrelated to question | Plausible method with some limits | Method directly answers question with prerequisites and limitations checked |
| Efficiency | Unbounded or excessive effort | Timebox/stop criteria exist | Smallest credible effort is selected and stop criteria protect decision quality |

A strong plan is not proof that the research was conducted or that conclusions are true.
