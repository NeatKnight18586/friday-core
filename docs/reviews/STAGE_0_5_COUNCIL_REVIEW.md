## 5. Critical caveats retained

- “Professional-grade” means methodologically faithful, proportionate, evidence-aware and inspectable; it does not mean the AI has professional credentials.
- The Agent Skills format does not guarantee identical discovery, permissions, tools or behavior across platforms.
- Desk research alone is not direct user validation.
- A provisional opportunity map is not presented as a fully evidence-grounded Opportunity Solution Tree when prerequisites are missing.
- A simulated Four-Council review is not four independent experts.
- NUTS cannot guarantee novelty, patentability or freedom to operate.
- No quality checklist guarantees truth; unresolved uncertainty must remain visible.
