# Interview Guide — [Research Round]

> Use only after the research purpose, participant selection and consent plan are approved. Do not store participant identities or recordings in this file.

## Opening and consent check
- Introduce researcher and purpose in plain language.
- Explain activities, duration, data collected, observers/recording, use/sharing, retention and withdrawal.
- Confirm consent before proceeding; confirm separate recording consent if relevant.
- Remind participant they may skip questions or stop.

## Recent experience (ask before presenting a solution)
1. Tell me about the last time you [situation/problem].
2. What were you trying to accomplish?
3. What happened from the beginning to the end?
4. What did you do when you encountered difficulty?
5. What tools, people, workarounds or alternatives did you use?
6. What was the consequence in time, effort, cost, risk or outcome?
7. What did you try previously?
8. Can you show an artifact or example, if comfortable and safe?

## Context and variation
- When does this happen and when does it not?
- Who else is involved or affected?
- What makes the process easier or harder?
- What accessibility, language, connectivity or resource constraints matter?

## Close
- What have I misunderstood?
- Is there an important perspective we missed?
- May we contact you for a follow-up, under the stated consent terms?
